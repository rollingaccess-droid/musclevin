FAQ

Is this a certified appraisal?

No. It's an informational report. If you need a value for insurance, probate, taxes, a loan or a court, hire a certified appraiser. Our report can help you get ready for that appointment.

Is the VIN decoder free?

Yes. The decoder on this page runs in your browser and does not ask you to sign up. It reads 1964-1980 Chevrolet, Pontiac, Ford, Mercury, Dodge, and Plymouth VINs. A full value report, when it opens, is a separate paid product. Join the waitlist if you want that.

Can a VIN prove my car is an SS or has a Hemi?

No. A VIN can't prove an option package. On many of these cars an SS, Hemi, Judge, or similar package was an option and is not its own VIN series. The decoder says when a VIN is only consistent with that kind of car. Proof takes the trim tag, a build sheet, or factory paperwork.

Does the free decoder send my VIN anywhere?

No. The decode runs in your browser. Joining the waitlist sends only the email address and the optional car description you submit.

Which cars do you cover?

The free decoder covers 1964-1980 Chevrolet, Pontiac, Ford, Mercury, Dodge, and Plymouth. Other makes, and 17-character VINs from 1981 on, get a plain message instead of a guess. The full report is aimed at American classic and muscle cars. If we can't give a useful answer, we'll say so.

Can you prove my car is "numbers matching"?

Photos and decoded codes can show whether the numbers are consistent. Proof often needs factory documents such as a Marti Report, PHS docs or a build sheet. We'll tell you which ones apply to your car.

Where does the value range come from?

From comparable sales of similar cars, adjusted for the condition and originality you describe. We show the range and how confident we are. We don't give one number.

I don't know anything about cars. Can I still use this?

Yes. Most people who need this inherited the car or are helping a relative. We'll tell you exactly which photos to take.

What do I need to send?

The VIN, a photo of the trim/cowl/fender tag if the car has one, and general photos. Paperwork such as titles, window stickers or build sheets helps but isn't required.
