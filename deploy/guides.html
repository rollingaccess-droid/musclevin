Classic VIN decoding guides

Step-by-step guides to reading pre-1981 muscle car VINs, one model at a time. Every code table comes from the published enthusiast references cited at the bottom of each guide, and where sources disagree, the guide shows both readings.

  • How to decode a 1969 Chevelle VIN
    Division, series, body style, year, plant, and sequence codes for the 13-character 1969 Chevelle VIN, with two worked examples.
  • How to decode a 1969 Camaro VIN
    Division, car line, 6-cylinder or V8, body style, year, plant, and sequence for the 13-character 1969 Camaro VIN, with a worked example.
  • How to decode a 1965-1973 Mustang VIN
    Model year, plant, body serial, year-by-year engine codes, and unit number for the 11-character first-generation Mustang VIN.
  • How to decode a 1965-1971 Pontiac GTO VIN
    Division, series, body style, year, plant, and sequence for the 13-character 1965-1971 Pontiac VIN, plus what changes in 1964 and 1972, with a worked example.
  • How to decode a 1969 Dodge and Plymouth B-body VIN
    Car line, price class, body, engine code, year, plant, and sequence for the 1969 Road Runner, GTX, Satellite, Super Bee, Coronet, and Charger, with a worked example.
