How to decode a 1969 Camaro VIN

A 1969 Camaro VIN has 13 characters. Read left to right, they give the GM division, the car line, 6-cylinder or V8, the body style, the model year, the assembly plant, and the car's sequence number at that plant. This guide walks through each part using the two published charts listed under Sources at the bottom. Where they disagree, both readings are shown.

If you just want the answer for your car, paste the VIN into the free decoder on the home page. It runs in your browser and doesn't upload the VIN.

Worked example: 124377N100001

chevy-camaro.com uses this VIN as its example for the 1967-69 Camaro VIN format [1]. Its year digit is 7, so it's a 1967. A 1969 Camaro VIN follows the same layout with a 9 in position 6.

Character 1: division

1 means Chevrolet [2].

Characters 2-3: car line and engine type

Ground Up's 1969 Chevrolet chart gives 2 in position 2 for Camaro (it writes the series as "2000 = Camaro") [2]. chevy-camaro.com reads the third character as the engine type for 1967-69 Camaros [1]:

Characters 2-3Meaning
23Camaro, 6-cylinder [1][2]
24Camaro, V8 [1][2]

What about the SS, RS, and Z/28? Neither source lists a separate VIN code for the SS, RS, or Z/28 on a 1969 Camaro [1][2]. A real Z/28 and a plain Camaro V8 coupe can start with the same 12437. The VIN alone can't prove the package. That takes the trim tag, the engine stamping, and factory paperwork.

Characters 4-5: body style

CodeBody style
37Coupe. Ground Up's Chevrolet body tables call 37 a 2-door sport coupe [2]
67Convertible. Ground Up calls 67 a 2-door convertible [2]

chevy-camaro.com describes this as the fourth digit: 3 for coupe, 6 for convertible [1].

Character 6: model year

9 means 1969 [1][2]. On the same format, 7 is 1967 and 8 is 1968 [1].

Character 7: assembly plant

CodePlant
NNorwood, Ohio [1][2]
LVan Nuys, per chevy-camaro.com [1]. Ground Up's 1969 Chevrolet plant chart lists L as Los Angeles, California [2]

chevy-camaro.com lists only these two Camaro plants [1]. Ground Up's plant list covers every 1969 Chevrolet line, not just Camaro [2], so a different plant letter on a Camaro VIN deserves a closer look.

Characters 8-13: sequence number

  • The last six digits go up by one with each car built at each plant [1].
  • The sources don't agree on a Camaro-specific starting number for 1969. chevy-camaro.com gives no 1967-69 starting number (its example just ends in 100001) and says 1970-71 Camaros started at 500000 [1]. Ground Up's 1969 Chevrolet chart lists 300001 for Nova and Chevelle, 700001 for Corvair and Corvette, and 100001 for all other lines [2], but it doesn't name the Camaro. Treat the 1969 starting number as unconfirmed by these two sources.

What the VIN can't tell you

The 1969 Camaro VIN gives 6-cylinder or V8, but not the engine size. It doesn't record option packages like the SS, RS, or Z/28, and it can't show whether the engine is original. It also can't detect a swapped or re-stamped VIN plate. For those questions you need the trim tag, the engine stamping, and factory paperwork.
